# Angular contact physics increment

The optional body.angular setting enables rotational dynamics on a dynamic primitive. Existing projects and bodies without that setting retain their translational solver and authored rotation. The inspector exposes initial angular velocity in radians per second (three components, each ±20) and exponential angular damping (0–10). Project parsing validates and retains those fields. The shared viewport applies the runtime quaternion to Babylon meshes in both editor play and exported games, without changing authored transforms.

The solver integrates normalized world-space quaternions, uses box inertia or uniform-sphere inertia, transforms inverse inertia into world coordinates, and computes normal and Coulomb-bounded tangential impulses from contact-point velocity. Dynamic contacts use the midpoint of the overlapping AABB faces as an approximate contact point. Static surface contacts use the center of the colliding face. Rotation updates use conservative rotated box bounds and are blocked if a candidate orientation overlaps a solid, other body, player or support. Uniform spheres retain their spherical extent while spinning; friction converts sliding velocity into rolling. Angular velocity is bounded to 80 rad/s after contact. Respawn restores the initial orientation and spin.

This is **not complete rigid-body/contact physics**: the existing axis-swept broad/narrow collision approximation remains, contact ordering is sequential, arbitrary convex/manifold collision, stable resting angular stacks, gyroscopic integration, true joint constraints and shape-specific contact geometry remain open. Blocking an overlapping rotation is intentionally conservative and dissipative. No new acceptance points are claimed.
